# Portfolio Redesign Creative Brief

## Project Overview

Redesign my entire web portfolio from the ground up to position me as a **senior UX and interaction designer**, as well as a **new media artist** with select exhibitions. The site must feel **modern, elegant, minimalistic**, and deeply intentional—borrowing thoughtfully from **Japanese design language**, without becoming cliché or ornamental.

The goal is to create a portfolio that feels unique, mature, and unmistakably crafted—not generic, not template-driven.

---

## Core Objectives

1. Present my UX and interaction design expertise at a senior level.
2. Integrate my identity as a new media artist in a subtle yet powerful way.
3. Blend modern minimalism with Japanese design sensibilities: restraint, whitespace, balance, asymmetry, tactility, poetic calm.
4. Create a portfolio that feels distinctive, premium, and artful.
5. Support both desktop and mobile with an equally refined experience.

---

## Visual Direction

### Overall Aesthetic

* **Minimal, clean, non-ornate**
* Heavy use of **negative space**
* **Soft, intentional typography**
* Layouts inspired by Japanese editorial design and architectural calm
* A feeling of **quiet sophistication**

### Japanese Design Influence (non-stereotypical)

* **Ma (間):** generous spacing that creates meaning
* **Wabi-sabi:** subtle texture, imperfect balance, gentle asymmetry
* **Shibui:** restrained elegance; refined without calling attention to itself
* **Kanso (簡素):** simplicity with purpose, not emptiness

### Color Palette

* Neutral base: off‑white, soft greys, warm black
* Accents: muted red or vermilion, dusty indigo, or deep forest green (designer to propose variations)
* Subtle gradients or textures may be used sparingly to evoke tactility

### Typography

* Mix of a modern sans-serif with a refined serif or Japanese-inspired grotesk
* Typography should feel editorial, calm, expertly spaced
* Emphasize hierarchy through weight, scale, and spacing—not color

---

## Interaction Style

* Subtle, poetic motion—never flashy
* Micro-interactions inspired by **physical gestures** or **paper-like transitions**
* Hover states that feel tactile (soft shifts, micro shadows, subtle parallax)
* Page transitions that feel cinematic but minimal—fade, slide in from soft edges, or dissolve
* Scrolling should feel intentional: frictionless, smooth, with occasional narrative moments

---

## Content Structure

### 1. Landing Page / Home

* A bold but understated hero message
* Possibly a signature statement reflecting both UX and art practice
* Soft motion or ambient art element in the background (optional)
* Immediate sense of calm, seniority, and intentionality

### 2. Work (UX + Interaction Design)

* Case studies with a strong editorial layout
* Mix of large imagery and refined typography
* A narrative approach: problem → insight → process → result
* Include motion demos if possible
* Each project should feel like a small magazine spread

### 3. New Media Art

* Showcase artistic works and exhibitions separately
* Grid inspired by Japanese print catalogs and exhibition posters
* Ability to show photos, videos, installation views
* Artist statements should feel poetic but concise

### 4. About

* Portrait in a minimal, understated style
* Clear senior-level language about experience and design philosophy
* Subtle integration of artistic background
* CV / exhibitions / achievements presented cleanly

### 5. Contact

* Minimal form
* Include light editorial elements or small visual signature

---

## Layout & Components

* Editorial-style grids (asymmetrical but balanced)
* Large margins and breathable spacing
* Clean custom navigation (horizontal or vertical)
* Sticky or floating nav that never feels heavy
* Modular system for case studies and exhibitions

---

## Technical & Functional Requirements

* Responsive across all breakpoints
* Accessibility first: contrast, type size, keyboard navigation
* Fast loading, optimized assets, no bloated libraries
* Support for video, subtle animations, and WebGL-based moments if lightweight

---

## Tone & Brand Personality

* Sophisticated
* Thoughtful
* Mature yet experimental
* Calm and precise
* Confident without being loud

---

## Deliverables Needed from Designer

1. **Full visual identity** (colors, typography, spacing rules)
2. **High-fidelity UI designs** for all pages
3. **Interaction and motion guidelines**
4. **Component library** for developer handoff
5. **Design system documentation**
6. Optional: **style frames or motion references** for transitions

---

## Notes for Designer

* The portfolio must feel *distinct*—avoid all clichés of modern designer portfolios.
* Limit the use of icons and decorative elements—let the typography and spacing carry the design.
* Think of this as designing a calm gallery or a minimal art book rather than a website.
* Japanese influence should be conceptual, not trendy.

---

## Success Criteria

* The final website feels premium, custom, and unique.
* It communicates UX seniority instantly.
* It integrates artistic identity seamlessly.
* Users feel a sense of calm, clarity, and intentionality.
* The design stands apart from template portfolios.

---

End of brief.
